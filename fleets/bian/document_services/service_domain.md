# BIAN Service Domain: Document Services

**Category:** Infrastructure  
**Enterprise-shared:** Yes — shared across all product fleets

## Purpose

Capture, classify, store, retrieve, and archive documents used in onboarding, credit, trade, and servicing.

## Canonical operations

Initiate, Update, Control, Retrieve, Execute

## Business objects

Document, DocumentType, ArchiveReference

## RAG rules

Dual-pull with enterprise document workflows; product policy stays in product fleet namespaces.
